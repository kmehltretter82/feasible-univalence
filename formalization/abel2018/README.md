# Abel 2018 & Feasible Univalence: Complete Lean 4.33.1 Formalization

This directory contains two complete, zero-`sorry`, constructive (`[propext]` / `[Quot.sound]` only, zero `Classical.choice`) Lean 4.33.1 libraries totaling **31,685 lines** across 30 modules (plus 2 executable check suites):

1. **`Abel2018`** (15 modules, 13,120 lines): Complete formalization of **M1** — Andreas Abel, Joakim Öhman, and Andrea Vezzosi, *Decidability of Conversion for Type Theory in Type Theory*, Proc. ACM Program. Lang. 2 (POPL 2018), Article 23, DOI [10.1145/3158111](https://doi.org/10.1145/3158111), matching the [v2.1 Agda artifact](https://github.com/mr-ohman/logrel-mltt/tree/eb2b15ab1c4b45923025834d3bb798943cb44b8e) (`eb2b15ab1c4b45923025834d3bb798943cb44b8e`), plus fuel-free deciders in `Decider.lean`.
2. **`FeasibleUnivalence`** (15 modules, 18,347 lines): Complete formalizations of **M2** ([arXiv:2603.29716v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29716v2) / ICFP 2023), **M3** (ICFP 2026, DOI [10.1145/3828677](https://doi.org/10.1145/3828677)), and **BC** (Bellantoni & Cook 1992 characterization of $\mathrm{FP}$ over deterministic multi-tape Turing machines, [`FeasibleUnivalence/BC/`](FeasibleUnivalence/BC/README.md), 7 modules, 4,442 lines), alongside research prototypes (**Paper A**, **Paper B**) and interface models (**Paper C & M11**, **Final Synthesis**; see [source-review.md](source-review.md) for evidential status and scope boundaries).

Both libraries use core Lean 4.33.1 only; no Mathlib dependency is required. The upstream MIT license is retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Part I: `Abel2018` — M1 Conversion Decidability (14 Modules, 12,979 Lines)

- [`Abel2018/Syntax.lean`](Abel2018/Syntax.lean) (211 lines): Raw de Bruijn terms (`Term`), order-preserving renamings (`Wk`, `wk`), parallel substitutions (`Subst`, `subst`), recursor step types (`recStepType`), numerals (`suck`), and syntactic `Neutral` / `Whnf` predicates (`Definition/Untyped.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/UntypedProperties.lean`](Abel2018/UntypedProperties.lean) (457 lines): Complete renaming and substitution algebra (`wk_comp`, `wk_subst`, `subst_wk`, `subst_comp`, `subst_beta`, `subst_beta_natrec`) (`Definition/Untyped/Properties.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/Judgments.lean`](Abel2018/Judgments.lean) (147 lines): Declarative typing (`CtxWf`, `TypeWf`, `HasType`), judgmental equality (`TypeEq`, `TermEq`), typed weak-head reduction (`TypeStep`, `Step`, `TypeStar`, `Star`, `TypeWhnf`, `TermWhnf`), and substitution typing (`SubstWf`, `SubstEq`) (`Definition/Typed.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/Weakening.lean`](Abel2018/Weakening.lean) (605 lines): Typed context weakening (`WkCtx`) and Lemma 2.7 (`wkTypeWf`, `wkHasType`, `wkTypeEq`, `wkTermEq`, `wkStep`, `wkTypeStep`, `wkStar`, `wkTypeStar`) (`Definition/Typed/Weakening.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/TypedProperties.lean`](Abel2018/TypedProperties.lean) (759 lines): Basic properties of typed reduction (Lemmas 2.2–2.5: `whnfRed*`, `whrDet*`, `subset*`, `redSubst*`) and the generic equality relation interface (`EqRelSet`, `synEqRel`) (`Definition/Typed/Properties.agda`, `Definition/Typed/EqRelInstance.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/Algorithm.lean`](Abel2018/Algorithm.lean) (153 lines): Executable fuel-bounded weak-head normalizer (`whnfFuel`) and six-mode conversion checker (`compareNeutralUp`, `compareNeutralDown`, `compareTypeUp`, `compareTypeDown`, `compareTermUp`, `compareTermDown`).
- [`Abel2018/Conversion.lean`](Abel2018/Conversion.lean) (1,116 lines): Inductive algorithmic conversion relations (`ConvNeUp`, `ConvNeDown`, `ConvTypeUp`, `ConvTypeDown`, `ConvTermUp`, `ConvTermDown`, `CtxEq`), Lemma 4.1 (`wkConv*`), fuel monotonicity (`whnfFuel_mono`, `compare*_mono`), `whnfFuel` adequacy on typed WHNF reductions (`whnfFuel_of_TypeWhnf`, `whnfFuel_of_TermWhnf`), and all-successful-run correctness propagation (`compareTypeUp_all_runs_correct`, `compareTermUp_all_runs_correct`) (`Definition/Conversion.agda`, `Definition/Conversion/Whnf.agda`, `Definition/Conversion/Weakening.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/LogicalRelation.lean`](Abel2018/LogicalRelation.lean) (1,164 lines): Kripke logical relation for reducibility parameterized by `EqRelSet`, constructed in Lean 4 without induction-recursion via an inductive semantic type code `LogRelKit : TypeLevel → Context → Term → Type` and recursive term relations (`LogRelAt`, `LogRel`) (`LogicalRelation.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/LogicalRelationProperties.lean`](Abel2018/LogicalRelationProperties.lean) (1,486 lines): Proof irrelevance / shape transfer (`irrelevance*`), weakening (`wk*`), escape (`escape*`), reflexivity (`refl*`), symmetry (`symm*`), transitivity (`trans*`), conversion (`conv*`), and WHNF expansion (`redSubst*`) (`LogicalRelation/*.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/Substitution.lean`](Abel2018/Substitution.lean) (1,446 lines): Semantic validity of contexts, substitutions, types, and terms (`ValidCtx`, `ValidSubst`, `ValidType`, `ValidTypeEq`, `ValidTerm`, `ValidTermEq`), plus weakening, lifting, and single-variable substitution validity (`LogicalRelation/Substitution/**/*.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/Fundamental.lean`](Abel2018/Fundamental.lean) (1,850 lines): Fundamental Theorem (Theorem 3.21: `valid`, `fundamentalType`, `fundamentalTerm`, `fundamentalTypeEq`, `fundamentalTermEq`), substitution validity (Theorem 3.23: `fundamentalSubst`, `fundamentalSubstEq`), and reducibility of well-formed types and terms (`reducibleType`, `reducibleTerm`, `reducibleTypeEq`, `reducibleTermEq`) (`LogicalRelation/Fundamental.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/Consequences.lean`](Abel2018/Consequences.lean) (918 lines): Syntactic validity (Theorem 3.24: `syntacticEq`, `syntacticTerm`, `syntacticEqTerm`), Π-injectivity (Theorem 3.26: `injectivity`), type constructor disjointness and WHNF agreement (Theorems 3.25 and 3.33: `U_ne_nat`, `U_eq_A`, `nat_eq_A_whnf`, `ne_eq_A_whnf`, `pi_eq_A_whnf`), substitution (Theorem 3.32: `substitution`, `substitutionTerm`), weak-head normalization (Theorem 3.28: `whNorm`, `whNormTerm`), closed-natural canonicity (Theorem 3.31: `canonicity`), and `0 ≢ 1` (Theorem 3.35: `zero_ne_one`) (`Definition/Typed/Consequences/**/*.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/Completeness.lean`](Abel2018/Completeness.lean) (1,451 lines): Soundness of algorithmic conversion (Lemma 4.3: `soundnessConvUp`, `soundnessConvUpTerm`, etc.), context stability (Lemma 4.4: `stability*`), symmetry and transitivity of algorithmic conversion (`sym*`, `trans*`), generic reflection/reification (`lift*`, `univConv*`, `convEqRel`), and Completeness of Algorithmic Equality (Theorem 4.11: `completeEq`, `completeEqTerm`) (`Definition/Conversion/Soundness.agda`, `Stability.agda`, `Symmetry.agda`, `Transitivity.agda`, `Lift.agda`, `Universe.agda`, `EqRelInstance.agda`, `Consequences/Completeness.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/Decidable.lean`](Abel2018/Decidable.lean) (1,216 lines): Uniqueness of inferred neutral types (`uniqueNeUp`, `uniqueNeDown`), mutual fuel adequacy and decidability of all six comparison functions (`compare*_adequate`), Decidability of Algorithmic Equality (Theorem 4.5: `decConv*`), Decidability of Judgmental Equality (Theorem 4.12: `dec`, `decTerm`), and the executable conversion adequacy theorems (`compareTypeUp_decides_typeEq`, `compareTermUp_decides_termEq`, `theorem_4_12_computational`) (`Definition/Conversion/Decidable.agda`, `Definition/Typed/Decidable.agda`).
- [`Abel2018/Decider.lean`](Abel2018/Decider.lean) (141 lines): Fuel-free deciders (not in the paper; closes item 5 of the external M1 review): constructive unbounded search `findNat` by well-founded recursion (no Mathlib `Nat.find`, no `Classical.choice`), `decTypeEq` / `decTermEq : … → Decidable (…)`, Boolean checkers `decideTypeEq` / `decideTermEq` with correctness theorems `decideTypeEq_iff` / `decideTermEq_iff`, and `theorem_4_12_decidable`.
- [`AlgorithmChecks.lean`](AlgorithmChecks.lean) (61 lines): Executable `#guard` regression tests and `#check` signature verifications for all M1 milestone and main theorems.

## Part II: `FeasibleUnivalence` — M2, M3, Bellantoni–Cook (`BC`), Papers A–C, and Final Synthesis (15 Modules, 18,347 Lines)

- [`FeasibleUnivalence/BC/`](FeasibleUnivalence/BC/README.md) (7 modules, 4,442 lines):
  - **Bellantoni & Cook (1992) Characterization of $\mathrm{FP}$**: Syntactic univariate polynomials (`BC/Poly.lean`), normal/safe function algebra `Fn n m` and Lemma 2.1 output-length bound (`BC/Syntax.lean`), multi-stack intermediate language (`BC/StackProg.lean`), deterministic multi-tape Turing machines and complexity class `FP` (`BC/TM.lean`), verified `Stmt`-to-`TM` compiler (`BC/CompileStmt.lean`), polynomial-time soundness `bc_soundness : BCDefinable f → FP f` (`BC/Soundness.lean`), and polynomial-time completeness `bc_completeness : FP f → BCDefinable f` & `defFun_eq_FP` (`BC/Completeness.lean`).
- [`FeasibleUnivalence/Modality.lean`](FeasibleUnivalence/Modality.lean) (1,268 lines):
  - **M2 Sections 2–3 & 6.1** (`Graded.Modality`, `Graded.Context`, `Has-well-behaved-zero`): Ordered semiring with meet (`Modality`), usage contexts (`Conₘ`) and semimodule laws, `HasWellBehavedZero`.
  - **M3 Sections 4.1 & 5** (`Subtraction`, `Natrec`, `Has-well-behaved-GLBs`): Canonical grade subtraction (`SubEq`, `SubLe`, `SupportsSubtraction`), recursion sequence `nrᵢ`, GLB characterization (`HasWellBehavedGLBs`), and the five concrete modalities (`ErasureGrade.erasureModality`, `ZeroOneMany.linearityModality`, `ZeroOneMany.affineModality`, `NatPlusInfinity.natPlusInfinityModality`, `SecurityLevel.securityModality`), plus the counterexample `nat_no_nr_glb` showing why plain `ℕ` without `∞` lacks `nrᵢ` GLBs.
- [`FeasibleUnivalence/Usage.lean`](FeasibleUnivalence/Usage.lean) (1,881 lines):
  - **M2 Sections 4–5** (`Graded.Usage`, `Graded.Substitution`, `Graded.Reduction`): Graded term syntax (`GTerm`, using curried eliminator branches aligned with `Abel2018`), erasure to `Abel2018.Term` (`toTerm`), modes (`Mode`), usage judgment (`Usage`, `γ ▸[m] t`), substitution matrices (`Substₘ`, `SubstUsage`, `Ψ ▶[m] σ`), weakening and substitution lemmas, **M2 Theorem 5.3 (`Usage.substₘ_lemma`)**, and **M2 Theorem 5.4 (`Usage.usagePresTerm`)** (including the GLB-based `natrec` fixpoint rule `Usage.natrec_fix` from M3 Section 5.2).
- [`FeasibleUnivalence/Erasure.lean`](FeasibleUnivalence/Erasure.lean) (2,723 lines):
  - **M2 Section 6** (`Graded.Erasure.Target`, `Extraction`, `LogicalRelation`, `Soundness`): Untyped CBV/CBN target language (`Strictness`, `TargetTerm`, `TargetStep`, `TargetStar`, `TargetStarNum`), extraction compiler (`erase`), commutation with weakening/substitution (`wk_erase_comm`, `subst_erase_comm`), **M2 Theorem 6.4 (`Usage.erased_not_hasX`)**, operational simulation (`erase_gstep_sim`), closed logical relation for erasure (`SemType`, `ErasedRel`, `ErasedRelGraded`), backwards closure and subsumption, **M2 Theorem 6.8 (`fundamental`, `fundamentalErased`)**, **M2 Theorem 6.9 (`soundness_nat`, `soundness_nat_canonical`)**, and the two scope counterexamples (`zero_divisor_breaks_erasure`, `erased_match_open_counterexample`).
- [`FeasibleUnivalence/HeapMachine.lean`](FeasibleUnivalence/HeapMachine.lean) (3,999 lines):
  - **M3 Sections 4–6** (`Graded.Heap.*`): Graded heap-and-stack abstract machine (`Entry`, `Heap`, `Cont`, `Stack`, `State`), resource-consuming lookup (`HeapLookup`), stack multiplicity (`Stack.mult` parameterized by `NatrecMultFn 𝕄 nr₂`), tracked (`HeapStep`, `⇾`), untracked (`HeapStepUntracked`, `⇢`), and full numeral (`HeapNumStep`, `↠`) transitions, state usage (`StateUsage` and extended `StateUsageExt` for `.sucₑ` frames), **M3 Lemma 4.1 (`lookup_succeeds`)**, **M3 Lemma 4.2 (`state_lookup_succeeds`)**, **M3 Theorem 4.3 (`usage_pres_step`, `usage_pres_star`, `numStep_not_pres_usage`, `usage_pres_numStar_empty`)**, **M3 Theorem 4.6 (`tracked_to_untracked_star`, `untracked_to_tracked_star`)**, **M3 Theorems 4.7–4.10 (`unfold_heapStar`, `unfold_heapStarUntracked`, `unfold_heapNumStar`, `initial_eval_numeral_sound`)**, and **M3 Theorem 6.1 (`SecurityLevel.non_interference`)**.
- [`FeasibleUnivalence/PaperA.lean`](FeasibleUnivalence/PaperA.lean) (1,609 lines):
  - **Paper A (Gates G2 & G3 — *Resource Correctness Survives Erasure*)**: Target closure-and-heap abstract machine (`TargetEntry`, `TargetCont`, `TargetStack`, `TargetHeap`, `TargetState`, `TargetHeapStep`, `TargetHeapNumStep`, `TargetHeapNumStar`), compiler (`compileHeap`, `compileStack`, `compileState`), simulation invariant (`ErasedHeapRel`, `ErasedStackRel`, `ErasedStateRel`), **Erasure Non-Access Theorem (`erased_slot_never_accessed`)**, **Lockstep Simulation (`erased_heap_lockstep_sim`, `erased_heap_star_sim`, `erased_heap_numeral_sim`)**, **Returned Closure Budget Theorem (`returned_closure_captures_budget`)**, **Main Theorem of Paper A (`paper_A_resource_correctness_after_erasure`)**, the Gate G2/G3 benchmark (`fold_capturing_closure_example`), and the 3 failure-test counterexamples (`cbv_erased_eval_counterexample`, `affine_duplication_counterexample`, `linear_discard_counterexample`).
- [`FeasibleUnivalence/PaperB.lean`](FeasibleUnivalence/PaperB.lean) (762 lines):
